# Opener examples

Described from the teardown cards. Use them for structure. Do not copy wording, footage or graphics.

## Mid-action over a running result

[@zubair_trabzada, DboqOSvEj_b](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DboqOSvEj_b/) · score 72 · 5.3M plays · 302x followers

- Says: "Jarvis, call the restaurant. Make a reservation for three people at 7 p.m. tonight."
- Text: none.
- Frame: top half is the assistant's HUD already live, bottom half is the creator at a mic.
- Why it works: the first words are spoken to the AI, not to the viewer. The viewer is watching a task start.

## Counted claim over the real system

[@bennett.spooner, DbcPo0dMQ3d](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbcPo0dMQ3d/) · score 70 · 1.6M plays · 14x followers

- Says: "I just built a team of 37 AI agents that run my entire company."
- Text: "37 AI Agent Company".
- Frame: handheld phone shot of a monitor showing the agent map, hand gesturing at it.
- Within 10 seconds he says the agents are real and clicks into one. The objection is handled before it forms.

## Mid-list, no intro

[@tenfoldmarc, Def9pj5TWtA](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Def9pj5TWtA/) · score 71 · 784K plays

- Says: the item names, rapid fire, starting on frame one.
- Text: the title with the count, plus the numbered list with the current item highlighted.
- Frame: he holds a phone-shaped frame in front of himself and a clip of each item plays inside it.

## Mid-conversation

[@juliaa.builds, DbeiibeoMe3](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbeiibeoMe3/) · score 68 · 216K plays · 32x followers

- Says: "Uh, pause? This guy's a freaking genius." Then: "Okay, let's finally connect Claude to Instagram."
- Frame: a reaction face layered over a handheld screen demo.
- The viewer joins an exchange already underway, then gets the topic in the second sentence.

## Flat claim, then the trigger

[@aaronautomates, DahhGBGJO6v](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DahhGBGJO6v/) · score 89 · 1.3M plays · 119x followers

- Says: "I've built the most ridiculous automation ever." Next breath: the trigger condition.
- Frame: close selfie, then straight into the scenario.
- One reel only for the story structure that follows, so treat it as promising, not proven.

## News framing with the verdict held back

[@itstundeanthony, Dd6izTYiln1](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd6izTYiln1/) · score 68 · 1.7M plays

- Says: the "someone just released" claim, with the number of platforms and "no API costs".
- Text: a "Fact or Hype?" pill at the top.
- Frame: creator with arms crossed, proof cards flashing.
- The opener sets a question the viewer has to stay for.
