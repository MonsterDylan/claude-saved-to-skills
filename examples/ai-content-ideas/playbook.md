# Example playbook: what 66 saved reels actually do

Source: one of my own saved collections, "AI content Ideas", harvested 2026-11-10.
70 links, 66 reels watched (4 were carousels), 60 creators, 54 minutes, 122.6M combined plays.
Full ranking: [scoreboard.md](scoreboard.md). Three of the 66 teardown cards are in `sample-cards/`.

## Read this first

- Score is 0 to 100 inside this collection. It blends reach past the creator's own followers, save plus share rate, and raw plays.
- Lift is how far the reels using a technique sit above or below the median of comparable reels.
- The collection splits in two. 49 **teach** reels (tutorials, tools, systems), median score 51. 17 **share** reels (skits, memes, showcases), median score 64. Lift is measured inside each group.
- This is correlation in a hand-picked set of 66. Most techniques have 5 to 15 reels behind them. Treat it as a strong hint, not a law.
- Score measures reach and saves. It does not measure leads or clients.

## The headline

**Raw beat polished.** The techniques that sat above the median are all about making the demo feel live. The ones that sat below are the heavily produced ones.

| Sat above the median | Lift | Creators |
|---|---|---|
| Start mid-action or mid-conversation | +20 | 7 |
| Demonstrate the technique on the video itself | +18 | 3 |
| Keep a face in frame during the screen demo | +16 | 8 |
| Back the claim with real headline or source screenshots | +13 | 3 |
| Put an obstacle, skeptic moment or failure inside the demo | +12 | 6 |
| Film the real screen with a phone instead of screen recording | +8 | 11 |

| Sat below the median | Lift | Creators |
|---|---|---|
| Name a specific profession in the first line | -32 | 6 |
| Use clean designed mocks in place of real footage | -25 | 5 |
| Cut back to your face for the ask | -25 | 8 |
| Put a visual on every spoken noun, changing every 1 to 3 seconds | -23 | 15 |
| Tour your real system one area at a time | -16 | 6 |
| Show the deliverable right before the ask | -16 | 6 |

## Hooks

- **Start mid-action** is the clearest lift (+20, 7 creators). The first words are the command to the AI, the first list item, or a line from a conversation already underway. Best example: [@zubair_trabzada](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DboqOSvEj_b/), "Jarvis, call the restaurant", 5.3M plays at 302x his followers.
- **Result first** is what everyone does (19 creators, lift 0). It is the default, not an edge.
- **Number plus outcome** (11 creators, +4) and **news framing** (8 creators, +8, just under the margin) lean positive.
- **Enemy hooks** (7 creators, +1) are neutral here.
- **Borrowed fame** (7 creators, -9) and **naming a profession** (6 creators, -32) sat below. The profession result is expected: it narrows the audience on purpose.

## Structure

- **Friction inside the demo** is a lift (+12, 6 creators). The 7 pm slot is taken. Someone asks "are you an AI?". The task fails without the tool, then works with it.
- **Numbered steps** are everywhere (18 creators) and neutral (-6).
- **A late bonus** ("here's the trick", "the crazy part") leans positive (+4, 8 creators).
- **Price anchor** (9 creators, -2) is neutral.
- **A system tour by area** sat below (-16, 6 creators). The one that worked, [@bennett.spooner](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbcPo0dMQ3d/), opened on a counted claim and proved in the first 10 seconds that the agents are clickable.
- **Promising, one reel only:** telling an automation as a story with a ridiculous payoff. [@aaronautomates](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DahhGBGJO6v/), score 89, 1.3M plays at 119x followers, 8.65% share rate.

## Edit

- **Face in frame during the demo** (+16, 8 creators) and **phone-filmed real screen** (+8, 11 creators) are the two edit lifts.
- **Panel on top, face below, captions on the seam** is the standard layout (17 creators, lift 0).
- **Word-by-word captions** are standard (16 creators, +4).
- **A visual on every spoken noun** is the most common heavy edit (15 creators) and sat well below (-23). **Designed mocks instead of real footage** did too (-25, 5 creators).
- Hard-cut count shows no pattern. Top third of teach reels: 6.5 cuts. Bottom third: 8.

## Length

Longer teach reels scored higher in this set. Top third median 63 seconds, bottom third 42 seconds. Reels over 60 seconds: median score 56.5 (18 reels). Reels of 45 seconds or less: 43.5 (22 reels). This cuts against "keep it under 50 seconds".

## CTA

- 39 of 49 teach reels use a comment keyword. As the last spoken line it is table stakes (32 creators, lift -2).
- The keyword buys comments, not reach. Keyword reels: 2.73% comment rate. Non-keyword teach reels: 0.16%. Their scores are about the same (51 vs 55).
- **Cutting back to the face for the ask** sat below (-25, 8 creators). Reels that kept the product on screen and pinned the keyword as a label did better.
- **No ask at all** is what every share reel does. Share reels reached a median 29.6x their followers. Teach reels reached 2.9x.

## Trust

- **Prove it is real** (9 creators, +4): click into it, open the file, show the call log.
- **Real source screenshots** (3 creators, +13): headlines, a README, at the moment of the claim.
- **Showing your own numbers** (9 creators, -3) is neutral.
