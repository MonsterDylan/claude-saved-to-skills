# Style sheets

How each creator's videos are put together. Use these for the discipline (zones, rhythm, restraint), and build in your own palette and type.

## @heystevetan

[DcarQfpTRt6](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DcarQfpTRt6/)

- **Canvas:** Warm, bright indoor talking head (presenter holding a fuzzy lav mic, white tee, colourful art and amber lights behind). Cuts alternate between a full-bleed face shot and a split layout with a graphic or screen recording in the top 50 percent and the face in the bottom 50 percent.
- **Zones:** Split layout: graphic or screen recording top 50 percent (about 340 of 680 px of each strip frame), caption bar at the seam at about 50 percent height, face bottom 50 percent. Full-face shots: caption at about 65 percent height over the chest. Full-screen cards (hook, 'HERE'S HOW YOU DO IT', CTA) take the whole frame.
- **Type:** Captions: clean sans in a dark rounded box, sentence case, whole phrase (3 to 5 words) per caption. Title cards: tall condensed heavy sans in caps (white or mustard) with a script-italic serif secondary line ('is like the new', 'comment', 'and I'll send you').
- **Captions:** Short phrase in a dark rounded box, replaced hard on each phrase, positioned at the seam between graphic and face in the split layout and at mid-chest on full-face shots. Active word is not marked.
- **Rhythm:** A new visual every 1 to 3 seconds. Face, B-roll, face, screen recording. The stages of the tutorial (open app, tap More, scroll, export, email, upload, result screens) each get their own screen capture, in order. The result screen is a long vertical document that slowly scrolls through the section the narration is on.
- **Default transition:** Hard cuts between face and graphics. Within a graphic scene: mostly hard swaps, plus a few blur-focus pulls (blurred then sharp) and a cross-dissolve for the document. Photo cards rotate and swap with a ring highlight.
- **Restraint:** No emojis, no sticker animations, no colour grading changes between face shots. Screen captures are kept fairly unedited. The face is always visible in the split layout. CTA is on a plain black card with just two lines.
- **Palette, by role:** hook card: deep red with orange glow border and gold type; 'how you do it' title card: black with white heavy type; app screens: near-black charcoal with green and red data accents; CTA card: pure black with white script and mustard-yellow keyword; caption: white text on a dark semi-opaque rounded box
- **Theirs, do not copy:** Whoop logo, app screens and band product shots; Photos of public figures; The mustard and red hook card palette and the 'is like the new' wording; The Claude interface screenshots

## @stilesai

[Dd35zfeq9bR](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd35zfeq9bR/)

- **Canvas:** Single continuous selfie webcam take, warm yellow room, creator centred behind a keyboard. The face is never replaced; graphics are laid over the top of the frame (ceiling and head area) and the footage is punched in or re-framed slightly between sections (head sits higher or lower in the frame).
- **Zones:** Top 3 to 25 percent of the frame, full width with a small side margin: one white card at a time, covering the ceiling and sometimes the top of the head. Face fills the middle 25 to 70 percent. Captions sit at about 60 to 65 percent height, over the chest. A black-on-white label 'Let your agents doomscroll' sits at the very top for roughly the first 12 seconds.
- **Type:** Cards: tiny uppercase monospace labels (corner tags like MY SAVED FOLDER, THE MEME, SKILL.md) plus a bold neutral grotesque sans for headings and big numbers, regular sans for list items, mono for code. Captions: bold neutral sans, sentence case.
- **Captions:** 1 to 3 words at a time, lower-middle of the frame, white bold, no highlight on the active word, each phrase hard-swaps to the next in time with speech (about every 0.4 to 0.8 s).
- **Rhythm:** One card at a time, a new one roughly every 3 to 8 seconds, each tied to a spoken beat (meme, 70 reels, checklist, skill file, You vs Claude, steps, comment prompt). Between cards the top of the frame is bare for about 0.6 s, which lets the face breathe. The cards form a story: 0 to 70 reels, Claude checks off transcripts/frames/captions, grid dims to the few kept, skill file types out, then a You vs Claude comparison.
- **Default transition:** Cards leave with a quick fade (about 0.2 to 0.4 s) or a hard cut in under 0.2 s, then there is a short empty gap, then the next card fades in (0.2 to 0.4 s) or pops in. Content inside a card changes by crossfade, line-by-line type-on, or block-by-block fill.
- **Restraint:** No sound-reactive or camera-shake effects, no colour, no icons or emoji, no animated backgrounds, no text behind the head, no zooms on the cards. Everything is flat black-and-white UI-style cards in a mono and grotesque pairing. The face is never covered by captions. Only one graphic is on screen at once.
- **Palette, by role:** card background: white with a thin black 1px outline, no rounded corners, no shadow; ink and blocks: black, plus light grey for 'inactive' or 'forgotten' blocks and mid grey for dimmed ones; only colour accents: tiny green dots and checkmarks, pink message bubble inside the meme screenshot; caption: white text with soft dark shadow, no box
- **Theirs, do not copy:** The 'Let your agents doomscroll' headline label and the meme screenshot (a specific meme and Claude UI crop); The skill names reel-edit-plan, stilesai-reel-script and reel-diagnosis and the 'edited with this skill' footer wording; The specific number 70 and the 'worth doing twice' wording; The creator's caption styling and the cap with a number on it

## @stilesai

[Dd9DCEZh3In](https://www.instagram.com/reel/Dd9DCEZh3In/)

- **Canvas:** Off-white paper background (about #F0F0EA) that holds all graphics. A handheld selfie take of the creator occupies the lower 60 percent of the frame during 'face' beats. During 'step' beats the face disappears completely and the graphic fills the whole frame, with the caption box staying at the same height.
- **Zones:** Two layouts that alternate. Split layout: graphic panel on the top 40 percent, caption box on the seam at about 38 to 40 percent height, face on the bottom 60 percent. Full-screen layout: graphic over the whole canvas, red step chip at the very top (about 3 percent), caption box still at about 38 percent. Handwritten red annotations are tucked in a corner of the graphic.
- **Type:** Caption box and labels: uppercase monospace, wide tracking. Card text: clean neutral grotesque sans. Annotations: red handwritten script ('wrong', 'never opened', 'save this one', 'not happening', 'already knows'). Pixel-art blue robot mascot with a held tablet that changes colour.
- **Captions:** A black box with white all-caps mono text, 2 to 4 words per phrase, centered near the face/graphic seam. The box hard-swaps text every 0.8 to 1.2 s with the speech. No per-word highlight and the box width follows the text.
- **Rhythm:** A graphic changes about every 1 to 3 seconds. The video is built in numbered sections: intro mascot, 'one reel = one skill' wrong diagram, sort into collections, bookmark, everything saved + comment keywords, DMs unread, STEP 1 / 2 / 3 in full-screen, setup stack, categories, not selling, DMs, comment CAMPAIGN, help request. Hard cuts between split and full-screen layouts mark the section changes. A 0.4 s face-only beat often sits between two full-screen steps.
- **Default transition:** Small graphics (cards, bubbles, bars) fade in over 0.2 to 0.4 s or pop in under 0.2 s, and stacked items append by sliding or fading in at the end of the list. Old graphics are removed by a hard cut in under 0.2 s. Layout changes between split and full-screen are hard cuts, not transitions.
- **Restraint:** No camera moves, zooms, glows, shadows, gradients or sound-reactive effects. Only flat shapes, one font family for the caption, red used only for step chips and hand notes. Face footage is never filtered or masked. The mascot never speaks and has no mouth. Three example keywords (GUIDE, PROMPT, SKILL) are shown in the comment-explainer card but the real call to action uses a single word, CAMPAIGN.
- **Palette, by role:** background: warm off-white; main accent and mascot body: saturated royal blue; secondary tile colours: periwinkle, black, charcoal grey, light grey, red; step chip and handwritten annotations: red (chip is white text on red); caption box: black rectangle with white text; highlights: yellow for the saved bookmark, green for FREE and KEPT chips
- **Theirs, do not copy:** The pixel-art blue robot mascot and its colour-changing tablet; The red handwritten annotation style and the specific words used; The exact SKILL.md, Launch reel and knowledge-base card designs; The caption box style (black mono all-caps) is a recognisable signature; The CAMPAIGN keyword and the 600 DMs line

## @tessa.fairbrook

[DdW5lIwzWiS](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DdW5lIwzWiS/)

- **Canvas:** Three base looks. (1) Cold open: overhead full-length shot of the speaker standing on a seamless light-grey floor. (2) Talking head: waist-up in front of a neon sign and plant, 9:16 full frame. (3) Between them, full-screen graphic scenes on a warm cream or white dotted/grid background with large grey sunburst-stroke shapes in two corners (top right, bottom left).
- **Zones:** Cold open: type fills the whole frame around a tiny figure. Tool segments: top about 50 percent is a screenshot or built UI panel, bottom about 50 percent is the speaker, with a salmon pill label sitting on the seam between them. Some segments cut the speaker away and go 100 percent graphic (cream scene). Outro and 'These can seriously upgrade' beats are full-frame face with captions in the lower third over a dark gradient.
- **Type:** Cold open: very condensed heavy grotesque, huge, mixed red and black, lowercase 'claude' in red. Title card: small geometric sans lowercase over big bold geometric caps in salmon, plus a black rounded 'PART 81' tag. Captions: bold geometric sans (white) paired with a salmon high-contrast serif for the emphasised phrase. Pills: bold sans on a salmon rounded rectangle.
- **Captions:** Two-line stacked captions in the lower third of the face shot: line one white sans, line two salmon serif. Phrases build in small chunks; letters slide up and blur in. Hey, is salmon, I'm Tessa, is white serif. A separate salmon pill on the panel seam shows the current spoken term (First contact, Second Chrome, dev tools MCP, Third super, base MCP, Fourth, Vercel MCP) and swaps word by word.
- **Rhythm:** A new shot or graphic about every 3 to 6 seconds. Each tool gets one cycle: cut to dark repo screenshot with speaker (pill on seam), then cut to a cream animated UI that demonstrates what the tool does, then back. Order repeats four times.
- **Default transition:** Hard cuts between scene types. Inside scenes, UI cards fade or slide in, text types or slides up letter by letter. Screenshots get a slow push-in or drift.
- **Restraint:** No sound-sync flashes, no shake, no zoom punches on the face. One accent colour. The speaker is never covered by the graphic, always split-screen. Graphics are built UI mockups (not screen recordings) for three of the four tools, real repo screenshots for the repo shots.
- **Palette, by role:** cold-open type: red and black; scene background: warm cream / off-white with faint dot grid; accent, pills, caption highlight: salmon / terracotta orange; dark panels (GitHub, query UI, docs): near-black; highlight marker on repo names: olive-yellow; caption base: white
- **Theirs, do not copy:** The 'Best Claude Use Cases, part N' series name and sunburst shapes; The salmon and cream palette and serif/sans pairing as a brand identity; The neon sign background in her room; Her exact mock product names (nova.app, Lumen) and UI layouts

## @chandlerintelligence

[De8SPjEAuV0](https://www.instagram.com/reel/De8SPjEAuV0/)

- **Canvas:** Locked-off, dim, warm-dark selfie shot of the presenter at a desk with a laptop, vertical 9:16. He sits right of centre. Everything else is layered onto this one shot.
- **Zones:** Face stays in the right 55 percent and lower two thirds. Top-left is a rounded-corner inset 'window' (about 20 percent width when small, about 55 percent width and 55 percent height when expanded) that plays a second video: the finished example edit being explained. Top 25 percent full width is used for prompt bars, tool logos, repo cards and big type behind his head. Captions sit at about 60 percent height, centred, below the chin line.
- **Type:** Captions: bold rounded grotesque sans, sentence case, 2 to 3 words per line. Title behind head: monospaced pixel / terminal sans, large, white. Example-video type: high-contrast serif italic and roman mixed, magazine style. UI cards use small neutral sans (Inter-like).
- **Captions:** Two to three words at a time, white bold with shadow, centred at about 60 percent height, swapped word-group by word-group with a hard replace. Active word is not marked except one red highlight box on 'every word' (about 15 s).
- **Rhythm:** Something new on screen about every 1 to 3 seconds. Each graphic is tied to the exact tool or technique being named in speech and is a live demo of that technique (text behind head while talking about text behind head). The inset window swaps content and size on each topic change.
- **Default transition:** Mostly pop or very fast (under 0.4 s) replace. A few things slide or scale in from the corner. The inset resizes between small and large with a scale-and-reposition move; the contents change on a hard cut inside it.
- **Restraint:** No music-visualizer, no emoji, no animated captions beyond the word swap. One accent colour family (green and gold) for the example video, everything else neutral. Graphics never cover his face; large cards sit above his head.
- **Palette, by role:** base footage: dim warm charcoal and brown; inset window background / example-video cards: deep forest green; example-video accent text: muted gold serif, green label chips; prompt bars and UI cards: dark charcoal bar with coral-orange send button; white or near-black cards for docs; captions: white with soft drop shadow, no box; highlight flash: red box on one caption word (once), olive-green on a title word

Some scenes are graphic-only with a serif italic caption at about 33 to 85 percent height depending on the scene.

- **Palette, by role:** scene backgrounds: off-white, cream, near-black navy, and a flat terracotta-orange; white with faint leaf shadow for the app screenshots; brand accent: terracotta orange (mascot, plugin icons, highlight boxes, 'PLUGINS' word); text: near-black on light scenes, white on dark scenes; key-word caption highlight: orange glow behind white words ('and more', 'sets', 'Plugin')
