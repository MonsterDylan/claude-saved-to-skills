# Reel openers

An opener is the first spoken line, the on-screen text, and the first frame, landing together in the first 3 seconds. This skill's job is choosing between openers using evidence: which kinds travelled in 66 real reels, and which did not.

The evidence is in `references/evidence.md` (every pattern, its lift, and links to the reels). Worked examples are in `references/examples.md`. Lift means points above or below the median of comparable reels, on a 0 to 100 score. It is correlation inside one hand-picked collection, so use it to rank options, not to promise results.

## The patterns, in evidence order

**1. Start mid-action.** Lift +20, 7 creators.
The first words belong to something already happening: the command you give the AI, the first item of the list, a line from an exchange underway. No greeting, no "so I built". It works because the viewer is dropped into a scene and has to stay to find out what it is.
Example: "Jarvis, call the restaurant. Make a reservation for three at 7 tonight." [DboqOSvEj_b, 5.3M plays, 302x followers]

**2. News framing.** Lift +8, 8 creators. Just under the margin, so treat it as a good default, not an edge.
"X just dropped", "Claude can now", with the logo or repo page on screen. Only use it when the thing is new. [Dd6izTYiln1, DcQfwZUDwtx]

**3. Number plus outcome, mirrored on screen.** Lift +4, 11 creators. Table stakes.
The count goes in the first sentence and in a label on frame one. "I just built a team of 37 AI agents that run my entire company." [DbcPo0dMQ3d, 1.6M plays]

**4. Result first.** Lift 0, 19 creators. Table stakes.
The first frame is the finished thing working. This is what nearly everyone does, so it is the floor. Combine it with pattern 1 or 3 instead of using it alone.

**5. Enemy.** Lift +1, 7 creators. Table stakes.
Name the wrong way or the overpriced thing, then promise the fix in the same breath. [Dd9DCEZh3In, DcQ_zLBo1gK]

**6. Stacked comparison.** Lift 0, 5 creators. Table stakes.
Two versions in one frame, both labelled, so the contrast is readable with no sound. Suits before and after, AI versus real. [DdmumCkvPqQ, 7.0M plays]

**7. Made with it.** Lift 0, 3 creators. Thin evidence.
Say the video being watched was made by the tool. Strong when it is true and visible in the first frame. [De8SPjEAuV0]

## Combine, do not pick one

The strongest openers in the collection stack two patterns. Mid-action over a running result (the Jarvis reel). A counted claim over the real system (the 37 agents reel). Aim for a spoken pattern plus a first-frame pattern.

## Cautions

- **Naming a profession in the first line** sat 32 points below (6 creators). It narrows the audience on purpose. If the goal is clients in that profession, that trade can be right. Say so when you offer one, and do not offer it as the reach option.
- **Borrowed fame** (a celebrity, a movie clip, another creator's footage) sat 9 points below (7 creators). It delays the point.
- The median teach reel got to its point in 4 seconds. If the opener needs more than one sentence before the viewer knows what this is, cut it.

## How to work

Draft wide first, then rank. Write at least 12 openers freely, in any style that fits the build, before looking at the patterns above. Then score each against the evidence and keep the best. The evidence is a filter for choosing, not a mould for writing. Used as a mould it narrows the options and loses the lines with the most personality.

## What to produce

The top 5, ranked, in this shape:

```
1. [pattern + pattern]
   Says:   the first spoken line, verbatim
   Text:   the on-screen text in the first 2 seconds
   Frame:  what the first frame shows, something they can film today
   Why:    one line, citing the evidence
```

Then:

- **Shoot this one:** your pick and the reason.
- **Cut, and why:** openers you drafted and dropped because of a caution above, one line each.
- **Worth keeping:** up to 5 more lines from the wide draft that are strong on wording even if the evidence does not rank them. Label them as unranked.

Across the top 5, at least two should start mid-action, since that is the best-evidenced lift. Use the person's real numbers. Where a number would help and none was given, leave a marked blank.

## Fit with the user's own rules

If the user has their own voice rules, hook bank or script skill, those govern wording. This skill supplies the evidence for which kind of opener to choose and what the first frame should be.
