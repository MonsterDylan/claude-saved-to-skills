# Demo reel examples

Beat by beat, from the teardown cards. Use them for structure. Do not copy wording, footage or graphics.

## The errand demo with friction

[@zubair_trabzada, DboqOSvEj_b](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DboqOSvEj_b/) · 74s · score 72 · 5.3M plays · 302x followers

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Mid-action open | Voice command to the assistant. It answers "Ringing the restaurant now". |
| 0:08 | Demo | The call plays on speaker. HUD shows ON THE CALL. |
| 0:22 | Friction | 7 pm is unavailable, 8 pm offered. The AI says it will check with its boss. |
| 0:34 | Report | It reports back. HUD shows the call summary. |
| 0:43 | Human decision | "8 pm works." It calls back. |
| 0:50 | Payoff | Booking confirmed, with a dry joke. |
| 1:10 | Ask | Plain end card pointing to the full tutorial. |

Layout: HUD on top, creator below, one uncut take, zero hard cuts. The phone visibly rings and the call is logged, so the viewer sees it was real.

## The counted claim, proved fast

[@bennett.spooner, DbcPo0dMQ3d](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DbcPo0dMQ3d/) · 86s · score 70 · 1.6M plays

| Time | Beat | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Hook | "37 AI agents run my entire company" over the live agent map. |
| 0:04 | Structure | Six departments named, camera moves in as he clicks each. |
| 0:12 | Prove it is real | Clicks into one agent and shows its skills and SOP panel. |
| 0:24 | Tour | Walks the departments. |
| 0:53 | Second reason to care | Preloadable personas others can use. |
| 1:21 | Ask | Back on the full map, keyword as an on-screen label, spoken last. |

Shot on a phone pointed at a real monitor, pushing in physically. No face at all. This is the one area-by-area tour in the collection that scored well, and it front-loaded the claim and the proof.

## The tool teaches itself

[@chandlerintelligence, De8SPjEAuV0](https://www.instagram.com/reel/De8SPjEAuV0/) · 85s · score 69 · 518K plays

Opens on the claim that the tool edited this video. Each of seven tips is applied to the reel as he explains it: text behind his head while he explains text behind the head, the grade changing while he explains the grade. Each tool or prompt appears as a card in the top third at the moment he names it, with his face still visible below. He holds the most important tip for 1:08 and labels it. The ask is the last line, with the keyword in type behind his head.

## The automation as a story

[@aaronautomates, DahhGBGJO6v](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DahhGBGJO6v/) · 60s · score 89 · 1.3M plays · 119x followers · 8.65% share rate

One reel, so promising and unproven. He narrates a workflow as trigger, consequence, consequence, absurd final action: surf forecast hits, flights and villa found, the group gets a poll, bookings confirm, a taxi is waiting, beers are in the fridge. A thin strip of the workflow stays on screen under the app screens so the viewer remembers it is automated. Returns to face for a one-keyword ask.

Note that this reel does put an image on every noun, the technique that sat below the median overall. Here each image is a step in a story, not decoration.

## Handheld setup tutorial with a face bubble

[@krudd.jr, DeHX34IxveJ](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeHX34IxveJ/) · 90s · score 66 · 508K plays

Result screenshots inside the first 10 seconds, then real screens filmed by hand with a finger pointing, and his face as a small cut-out bubble in the corner throughout. Big word-by-word captions in the lower middle so both stay readable. Keyword label stays on screen for the final seconds.
